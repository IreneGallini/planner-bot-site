Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Planner Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Free/busy times and event details from the owner's Google Calendars (read-only), used to find free time and to summarise the day.
  • Events on a dedicated "Planner" calendar, which the app creates, updates, and deletes to reserve time blocks.

How data is used and stored

Calendar data is used only to plan the owner's schedule. It is stored on a private server controlled by the owner, and is not reachable from the public internet. It is processed by a language model running on that same server. It is not sent to third parties.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train models.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's database removes all stored data.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/IreneGallini/planner-bot-site.
